FEATURES

Everything a custom cursor should be, and nowhere it shouldn't.

Per-app cursors

The custom cursor shows only while you hover the chosen app and switches back the moment you leave it. Discord is the default.

A cursor for every state

Set different images for links, text fields, loading, resizing and more. Supports .png, .cur and animated .ani files.

Click effects

There are ten animations with their own color for left and right click. Adjust size and speed, or turn them off completely.

Zero lag

CursorSwap swaps the real Windows cursor instead of drawing a fake one on top, so there's no delay and no double cursor.

Starts with Windows

With one toggle, CursorSwap launches quietly in the system tray when you log in and stays out of your way.

Always restores

Your normal cursor comes back when you leave the app, quit CursorSwap or even if it crashes.

CURSOR STATES

Hover a link, a text box or a loading screen. Each gets its own look.

Pick an image for each tile. States you leave empty can use your main cursor or keep the normal Windows one. Right-click a tile to set exactly where the click point is.

GET STARTED

Up and running in three steps.

  1. 1

    Download & run

    Grab CursorSwap.exe from the release page and open it. There's nothing to install.

  2. 2

    Pick your app

    Discord is set by default. To use another app, type its name, choose it from the list or use Pick window.

  3. 3

    Choose cursors & effects

    Drop in your cursor images and pick your click effects. Hover the app and it just works.
